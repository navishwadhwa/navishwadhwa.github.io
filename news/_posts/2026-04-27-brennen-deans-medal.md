Brennen Wise has been awarded the Department of Physics Dean's Medal — one of the highest honors given to graduating seniors at ASU. Brennen is graduating this spring with a Bachelor's in Biophysics and a minor in Statistics from Barrett, The Honors College.

ASU News featured Brennen in a [profile piece](https://news.asu.edu/20260427-sun-devil-community-phdbound-graduate-takes-pride-multidisciplinary-research) highlighting his multidisciplinary research and his path to graduate school. This fall, Brennen will begin a PhD at Yale. Congratulations, Brennen!
