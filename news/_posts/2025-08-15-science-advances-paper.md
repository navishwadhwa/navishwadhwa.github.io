A collaborative paper with the Shepherd lab is now published in [Science Advances](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adr8004){:target="_blank"}. The work presents Fourier synthesis optical diffraction tomography, a new technique for volumetric imaging at kilohertz rates. Congratulations to Luis Meneses for his contributions to this work!
