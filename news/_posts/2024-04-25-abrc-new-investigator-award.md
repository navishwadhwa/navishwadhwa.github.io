The lab has been awarded a New Investigator Award from the [Arizona Biomedical Research Centre](https://azdhs.gov/azbrp/index.php){:target="_blank"} (ABRC). This $225,000 grant will support our project "Uncovering the role of flow sensing in urinary tract infections." We are excited to pursue this new research direction!
