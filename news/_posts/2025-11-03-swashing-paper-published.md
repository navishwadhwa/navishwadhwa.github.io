Our paper on swashing motility is now published in the [Journal of Bacteriology](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jb.00323-25){:target="_blank"}! This work, led by Eric Dudebout in our lab and Justin Panich from the Blair lab at the University of Utah, describes a propulsion-independent form of bacterial surface migration in *Salmonella* and *E. coli* — bacteria moving across surfaces without using their flagella. Congratulations to Eric!
