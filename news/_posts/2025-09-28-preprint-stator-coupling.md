A new preprint from the lab is up on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.28.679083){:target="_blank"}! Led by PhD student Shabduli Sawant, in collaboration with Banu Ozkan's group at ASU, this work shows that long-range allosteric coupling between stator units regulates the output of the bacterial flagellar motor. Congratulations to Shabduli on this milestone!
