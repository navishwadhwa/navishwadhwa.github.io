Our paper on osmotic stress in bacteria is now published in the [Biophysical Journal](https://www.cell.com/biophysical-journal/home){:target="_blank"}! Led by Luis Meneses, this work shows that osmotic stress triggers a fast and reversible collapse of the proton motive force in *Escherichia coli*. Congratulations to Luis, Farhad, Eric, and all co-authors!
