A collaborative review paper is now published in [Microbiology and Molecular Biology Reviews](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/mmbr.00092-22){:target="_blank"}! The paper, co-authored with the groups of Marc Erhardt and Philipp Popp at Humboldt University Berlin, provides a comprehensive overview of how the bacterial flagellum is built — covering regulation, dynamic assembly, and function. PhD student Shabduli Sawant is a co-author. Congratulations Shabduli!
