We are thrilled to be part of a new NIH R01 grant awarded to the Shepherd lab at ASU! The project, "Life in slow motion: Fourier synthesis optical diffraction tomography," will develop a powerful new imaging platform for studying bacterial communities in three dimensions at kilohertz rates.
