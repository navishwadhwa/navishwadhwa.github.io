Spring 2025 has been a great season for the lab! Brennen Wise won the **Rick and John Jacob Award for Undergraduate Research** from the ASU Physics department and the **Best Poster Award** at BioPhest. Shabduli Sawant was recognized with the **Outstanding Graduate Student Research Award** from the School for Engineering of Matter, Transport and Energy, and also received the Agouron Institute Travel Award and the Biodesign Travel Award. Seiga Yanagisawa won the **People's Choice Award for best flash talk** at the Biodesign Fusion Retreat. Eric Dudebout received both the ASU Graduate Student Government Travel Grant and the ASU Graduate College Travel Award. So proud of everyone!
